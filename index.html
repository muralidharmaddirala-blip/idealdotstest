About Ideal Dots

A small studio with a loud portfolio.

Ideal Dots started with a simple idea: publicity design should feel less like noise and more like connection. For over a decade we've partnered with startups and established brands alike to build identities and campaigns that people actually stop to look at.

We're strategists, designers, and storytellers who believe the best publicity isn't loud for the sake of it — it's precise, memorable, and exactly on brand.
